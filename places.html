Places to check out in your next vist

New York is well known for it's numorous places of tourism, but which of them is best for you? Click on any place in the map below and check it out! Read the info on the the side for faster knowledge!

George Washington Bridge

A famous suspension bridge connecting Manhattan and New Jersey, offering impressive views of the Hudson River and New York City skyline.

Empire State Building

One of New York City’s most iconic landmarks, the Empire State Building is a historic skyscraper known for its stunning city views from its observation decks.

Bronx Zoo

One of the largest zoos in the United States, featuring hundreds of animal species and immersive exhibits across a vast urban park.

Flushing Meadows–Corona Park

A large Queens park known for its open green spaces, recreational areas, museums, and the iconic Unisphere.

Statue of Liberty

A world-famous symbol of freedom and one of New York’s most recognizable landmarks, standing on Liberty Island in New York Harbor.

Coney Island

A lively seaside destination in Brooklyn, famous for its beach, boardwalk, amusement rides, and classic New York atmosphere.
